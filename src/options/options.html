    Help

    How it works

    The extension hooks the microphone that WhatsApp Web asks for and passes it through a voice-effects engine running inside your browser. WhatsApp receives the changed voice for calls and voice messages.

    All processing happens locally on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and the extension makes no network requests.

    Send a song or audio file as a voice note
    1. Open WhatsApp Web, click the round 🎵 button on the right edge (or "🎵 Send audio file as voice note" in the toolbar popup), choose or drop an audio file, trim it if you like, and press Arm for next voice note.
    2. Open any chat and click WhatsApp's own 🎤 microphone button. The file starts playing into the recording by itself.
    3. When the panel says it has finished, press WhatsApp's Send ➤.
    • The other person sees and hears it as a normal voice message, with the usual waveform bubble.
    • Voice notes are mono and compressed for speech, so music sounds like a phone recording, not like the original file.
    • Your microphone is muted during that recording. Turn on Include my microphone to sing or talk over the track (karaoke), and Apply my voice effect to run the file through your current voice.
    • Works with MP3, M4A/AAC, WAV, OGG/Opus, FLAC and the audio of WebM/MP4 files, up to 30 minutes / 150 MB. The file never leaves your computer except inside the voice note you send.
    Tips
    • Wear headphones, especially when using "Hear myself".
    • Start from a preset, then fine-tune the parameters.
    • Lower Effect mix for a more subtle, believable change.
    • Use the mic test lab and Record 5 s to hear what the other side hears.
    Troubleshooting
    • Reload the WhatsApp Web tab after installing or updating the extension.
    • It works only on web.whatsapp.com in Chrome or Edge, not in the WhatsApp desktop or phone app.
    • If the other side hears nothing, turn the extension off with the master switch (your normal voice passes through) and report the problem.
    • If the status shows an error, reload the WhatsApp tab and start the call again.
    Privacy

    Your voice never leaves your device except through WhatsApp itself. Settings and custom presets are stored locally in your browser. Test recordings exist only in this page until you close it or download them.
